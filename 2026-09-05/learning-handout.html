From Grievance to Declaration

抓住两条线：作者怎样组织文章；事件怎样发展。

1. 两种 function，两个观察角度

Paragraph function 看写法：这一段对整篇文章有什么贡献？先概括内容，再用准确动词说明作者在做什么，例如 introduces, explains, contrasts, gives evidence, concludes。

Event-chain function 看发展：这一段中的事件在事情发展过程中起什么作用？分清 cause, response, change, result, impact，并找到前后联系。

Paragraph meaning 不等于 paragraph function。
“It is about the Declaration.” 只报话题。
“It explains how the Declaration organizes its argument.” 才说明这一段在文章中的工作。

一个段落可以解释某个 result；因此，它的 paragraph function 是 explanation，事件角色却是 result。两者不冲突。也不是每段都必须对应一个新事件。

蓝色词可点开查看词义。先读整段，再联系前后段。

3In July 1776, the Continental Congress adopted the Declaration of Independence. The document did more than announce a decision. A long list of then served as evidence that British rule had violated those principles. Finally, the declared the colonies free and independent states with the power to form alliances and govern themselves.

4The order of these parts reveals the document's public purpose. The Declaration addressed the king, the colonists, and audiences beyond North America. It to justify resistance, unite supporters, and make foreign assistance more likely. A reader who follows the sequence—principle, evidence, conclusion—can understand the argument without memorizing every date or complaint.

3. Worked model · 同一篇文章的两张图

以下是已经展开的示范。段落功能要同时说出动作和内容，事件角色要说出变化及其依据。

段Paragraph functionEvent-chain roleText-based explanation
1Introduces the grievances and the initial response.Cause / backgroundNew pressure led to boycotts and petitions seeking correction, not immediate separation.
2Explains the shift from reconciliation toward separation.ChangeFailed petitions and widening conflict made separation more believable; opinion remained divided.
3Explains the Declaration’s structure and argument.ResultCongress adopted a formal declaration. The rest of the paragraph explains how that result was presented.
4Explains the document’s public purpose and intended influence.Significance / intended impact of the resultThe document sought to justify resistance, unite supporters, and encourage foreign assistance.

第三段：不要把两个问题混成一个

What does the paragraph do?
It explains how the Declaration presents principles, evidence, and a formal decision.

What role does its main event play?
The adoption of the Declaration marks the formal result of the movement toward separation described earlier.

证据：“Congress adopted” 指向事件；“Its introduction … Its preamble … A long list … Finally …” 指向解释结构。

第四段的边界：“sought to” 和 “make … more likely” 表示目标及预期影响。不能改成 “all colonists united” 或 “foreign help was secured”。文中没有证明这些结果已经发生。

4. 文章结构，与文献内部结构

上表的 1–4 是这篇阅读文章的四段。第三段又介绍了 Declaration 内部的不同部分；这是另一层结构。

Document partJob理解重点
IntroductionExplains why separation needs public justification.交代为什么需要公开说明分离的理由。
PreambleStates principles about legitimate government.先提出判断政府是否正当的原则。
List of grievancesPresents evidence that those principles have been violated.列举申诉，支撑前面的论证。
Resolution of independenceStates the formal decision to separate.在原则与证据之后，作出正式决定。

这里的 resolution 是正式决议。它不是后来的战争胜利，也不能只理解为一个普通“解决办法”。

5. 把方法带到新阅读

  1. Job：题目问作者如何写，还是事件为何发生、改变了什么？
  2. Map：先用一句话概括本段；把写法与事件发展分开标。
  3. Evidence：回看前后段，圈出变化词、因果词和关键动作。
  4. Limit：保留范围。some 不是 all；sought 不是 achieved；先后顺序不自动证明因果。
  5. Answer：对比所有选项。淘汰只复述细节、套错段落功能、倒转因果或夸大结果的选项。

One-sentence passage summary

The passage explains how colonial resistance developed into a public declaration of independence and how the Declaration organized and justified that decision.

一句话保留整篇发展的方向与目的，不堆列每一个年份或法案。

6. 本课词义边界

  • Reconciliation / independence：前者修复关系，后者脱离原有政治控制；目标不同。
  • Consent / agreement：意义可以重叠；consent 常强调给予许可或认可，agreement 范围更广。在 consent of the governed 中，要读出被治理者的认可。
  • Sovereign / sovereignty：sovereign 可作形容词表示独立自主，也可作名词表示统治者；sovereignty 是主权这个抽象名词。
  • Seek / sought：sought 是过去式；sought support 只说明寻求支持，未保证获得支持。

先问“作者在做什么”，再问“事件起什么作用”。最后用原文限制答案。
