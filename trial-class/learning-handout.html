Author's Purpose

Purpose = Author's Action + Topic

  • explain / describe：说明事实、过程或特点
  • persuade：推动读者接受一个观点或行动
  • narrate：讲述事件或经历

先看每段的作用，再决定整篇文章在做什么。只有主题、只有一个细节、或加入作者没有表达的情绪，都不是完整目的。

Read and Map

Returning the Night Sky

INTRODUCEWhen the town of Bellwether opened a new community observatory, visitors were surprised by how few stars they could see. The nearby streets were safe and busy, but hundreds of uncovered lamps sent light upward as well as downward. That scattered glow, called light pollution, brightened the sky and made faint stars disappear. It also caused migrating birds, which often travel by natural light, to circle above the town longer than usual. For the observatory, this was more than disappointing: public programs depended on viewing dim objects, not only the brightest planets.

RESPONSEThe town council did not simply switch off every lamp. Instead, it tested shielded fixtures that directed light toward sidewalks. Warmer bulbs replaced several harsh blue-white ones, and timers dimmed empty parking areas after midnight. Engineers kept brighter lighting at crosswalks and near the hospital entrance, where clear visibility mattered most. During the six-month trial, residents reported no rise in nighttime accidents, while the observatory measured a darker sky over the central district.

LIMIT & RESULTThe project did not remove every source of unwanted light. Store signs and private floodlights still produced glare, and cloudy nights remained brighter than clear ones because clouds reflected the town's glow. Even so, the trial showed that careful design could reduce wasted light without leaving important places dark. Bellwether has since offered small grants to businesses that replace poorly aimed fixtures, hoping that many modest changes will make the night sky easier to see. The council also publishes a map of darker viewing areas so teachers can schedule astronomy events where the improvement is easiest to notice.

Example

1. The main purpose of the passage is to

  1. (A) persuade every town to turn off all outdoor lighting.
  2. (B) narrate the career of an astronomer who studied migrating birds.
  3. (C) explain how one town reduced light pollution while protecting important uses of light.
  4. (D) compare several species that travel by natural light.
  5. (E) criticize residents for caring more about safety than the night sky.

Answer: C

Evidence / Connector: Paragraph 1 introduces the problem, paragraph 2 explains the town's response, and paragraph 3 gives limits and results.

The answer names the author's action (explain), the topic (the town's lighting changes), and the passage-wide result/boundary.

Closest trap: A: it shares the lighting topic but exaggerates the action.

Fiction Evidence

  • Character Motivation：看人物想保护、获得或避免什么，用行动、对话和变化证明。
  • Inference：把两个以上的事实连接起来，但不加入原文没有支持的故事。
  • Tone / Attitude：看描写、动作和结尾带来的整体感觉，不只看一个情绪词。
  • Specific Detail：回到局部证据，区分原文事实和合理但未出现的内容。

The Unfinished Lantern

Every October, the people of West Harbor carried handmade lanterns along the river path. Jonah had joined the walk many times, but this year his grandmother asked him to repair the lantern that had hung in her attic for decades. Its wooden frame leaned to one side, and most of its paper panels had faded. One blue panel was torn almost in half. Jonah imagined how much better the lantern would look with smooth orange and gold paper, so he bought enough new sheets to replace everything.

At the kitchen table, Jonah removed the old panels while his grandmother sorted pieces of wire. When he lifted the torn blue paper, she stopped working. Her fingers rested briefly on two tiny initials written in one corner. Then she turned back to the wire and said only, 'Your grandfather always chose that blue.' Jonah looked again. He had planned to throw the panel away, but instead he placed it beside the new paper.

The next afternoon, Jonah repaired the frame and attached the orange and gold panels. Last of all, he spread the blue piece flat. He pasted a thin sheet of pale paper behind it so the tear would hold, then returned it to the lantern. The blue did not match the brighter colors, and one of the old initials was still difficult to read. Jonah decided that this was all right. He did not tell his grandmother what he had done.

On the night of the walk, hundreds of perfect circles of light moved beside the dark river. Jonah's lantern still leaned a little, and its patched panel glowed more softly than the others. For a moment, he wished he had made it completely new. Then his grandmother noticed the square of blue. Her face brightened, and she touched Jonah's shoulder without speaking. Jonah slowed his steps so they could carry the lantern together. Its uneven light no longer seemed like a mistake; it seemed like something that had found its way home.

Synonyms

Define先说出最简单的意思
Context想一个熟悉的短语或句子
Direction判断褒贬、强弱和程度
Test逐项检查“近义”而不是“相关”
Example

9. RELUCTANT:

  1. (A) willing
  2. (B) hesitant
  3. (C) silent
  4. (D) careless
  5. (E) ordinary

Answer: B

Evidence / Connector: Context: 'reluctant to speak' means not eager or willing to speak.

Hesitant is the closest meaning. Willing has the opposite direction; other choices describe different qualities.

Closest trap: C: a reluctant person may stay silent, but silent is a possible result, not the meaning.

Last Check

  • Reading：先标段落作用，再用原文锁定答案。
  • Purpose：用 action + topic 覆盖整篇文章。
  • Synonym：找“意思最接近”，不是“经常一起出现”。
  • Analogy：同一个具体 connector 必须在同一方向成立。
