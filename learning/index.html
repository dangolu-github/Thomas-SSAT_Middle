Learning System

Know the task before doing the task.

每个模块先学知识、题型和方法，再进入 guided practice 和独立诊断。

ReadingVerbalWriting
Reading

Text Map + Question Job + Evidence Lock

Read for paragraph roles, identify what the question asks, then limit the answer to exact passage evidence.

  • Main idea and purpose
  • Specific detail and structure
  • Inference, tone, and argument
Verbal

Meaning precision and relationship mapping

Synonyms and Analogies remain separate. One route tests exact word meaning; the other tests relationship and direction.

  • Meaning, nuance, morphology, and elimination
  • Bridge sentence, direction, degree, and option testing
Writing Sample

Plan + Develop + Revise

Practice Middle Level creative and personal prompts as school-facing writing, even though the sample is not scored.

  • Prompt interpretation and planning
  • Organization and development
  • Language control and revision
