Words for Investigation

specimen n.

a sample examined or studied

The specimen was placed on a clean slide.

供观察或研究的样本

magnify v.

to make something appear larger

The lens can magnify a tiny mark.

放大影像

transparent adj.

allowing light through so that objects can be seen clearly

We could see the water through the transparent container.

透明的

distinguish v.

to recognize the difference between things

Shape helped us distinguish the two leaves.

区分

detect v. [detective novel]

to discover or notice something present

The instrument can detect a small change.

发现或察觉

variable n.

a factor that can change in an investigation

The amount of light was one variable in the test.

变量

consistent adj. [steady, stable, constant]

following the same pattern without important variation

Her measurements were consistent across three trials.

Consistent results

保持一致的

infer v. [inference -- evidence / clues]

to reach a conclusion from evidence

We can infer that someone visited from the fresh footprints.

Differentiate A from B 把A和B区分开

依据证据推断

tentative adj. [uncertain/ provisional/~hesitant]

not final or certain and open to change

The explanation remained tentative until further tests.

暂定的；尚不确定的

replicate v. duplicate-

to repeat a study or procedure to check its result

Another group tried to replicate the study.

复制一份，做出一模一样的副本，强调copy

replcitate 复现（按相同的方法再做一次，看同一个结果能不能再次出现，证明之前的结果是不是可靠的）

重复实验或步骤以复核结果

Word Family

detect (v.) · detection (n.) · detectable (adj.)

The sensor can detect a change. The detection of a change takes time. A large change is easily detectable.

Synonyms

Action and Object

说清动作怎样作用于对象：例如剪去部分，不能只说“对它做某事”。

Worked example

Prune is to shrub as

  • (A) trim is to hedge
  • (B) harvest is to crop
  • (C) water is to seed
  • (D) uproot is to tree
  • (E) plant is to bulb

答案 A。Meanings：先确认首对词义。Connector：To prune a shrub is to cut away some of its growth; to trim a hedge is to cut away some of its growth. Order：保持左词到右词。Test：把全部选项代入。Limit：按下列差异排除。

  • A：prune 和 trim 都从植物上剪去部分枝叶以调整生长或形状。
  • B：harvest 收获作物，重点是取得产物。
  • C：water 给植物添加水，不剪去部分。
  • D：uproot 将整株连根移除，不修剪部分。
  • E：plant 把球茎种下，不处理已长成植物的部分。

最近干扰：D：移除整株与剪去部分不同。

Part and Whole

先区分实体部件、结构单元与类别成员，再检查方向。一个物体在另一个物体上，并不说明它属于其组成部分。

Worked example

Lens is to microscope as

  • (A) beaker is to container
  • (B) specimen is to slide
  • (C) valve is to pump
  • (D) telescope is to lens
  • (E) scientist is to laboratory

答案 C。Meanings：先确认首对词义。Connector：A lens is a working component of a microscope; a valve is a working component of a pump. Order：保持左词到右词。Test：把全部选项代入。Limit：按下列差异排除。

  • A：beaker 是 container 的一种，不是其内部部件。
  • B：specimen 被放在 slide 上，不是 slide 的工作部件。
  • C：lens 是 microscope 的工作部件；valve 是 pump 的工作部件。
  • D：telescope 包含 lens，方向反了。
  • E：scientist 在 laboratory 工作，不是器物的组成部件。

最近干扰：D：关系接近，但从整体到部件。

Tool and Function

分清工具执行的过程、直接测量的量，以及仅帮助完成的活动。用于计算某个量，不一定直接测量该量。

Worked example

Microscope is to magnification as

  • (A) thermometer is to temperature
  • (B) ruler is to length
  • (C) filter is to purification
  • (D) telescope is to astronomer
  • (E) lens is to glass

答案 C。Meanings：先确认首对词义。Connector：A microscope is used for magnification; a filter is used for purification. Order：保持左词到右词。Test：把全部选项代入。Limit：按下列差异排除。

  • A：temperature 是被测量的量，不是仪器执行的过程。
  • B：length 是被测量的量，不是过程。
  • C：microscope 用于 magnification；filter 用于 purification，都是工具和主要工作过程。
  • D：astronomer 是使用者。
  • E：glass 是材料。

最近干扰：A：测量对象与工作过程不同。

Observation and Explanation

Observation 记录看见或测得的现象；explanation 说明可能的原因。Evidence 是支持或限制某个解释的信息。准确记录现象，不代表已经确定原因。比较两种条件时，应检查还有哪些因素一起改变。

The Second Sample

1 At a school science club, Maya collected a of pond water in a jar. The water looked almost clear, but a microscope could details that her eyes alone could not . Through the lens she saw green specks mixed with pale fragments. She used their shapes to one group from the other. Sentence Core and Logic

Sentence Core · Modification / Reference · Logic · Meaning

先找主语与核心动词；括出修饰部分并确定指代；标明原因、对照或结论；最后用英文说完整意思。被动 be + past participle 把承受动作的一方放在主语位置。that/which 引导的定语从句可说明是哪一个对象；不能只把最近的名词当答案。

Sentence 1

Because the jars had received different amounts of light and had been kept at different temperatures, the comparison could not separate the effects of the two conditions.

主干 the comparison could not separate the effects；Because 从句给出原因；had received 与 had been kept 共用主语 the jars，前者主动、后者被动。两项条件同时变化，所以比较不能分离两者作用。不要将 because 后面的内容读成比较结论。

The comparison could not show the separate effect of light because both light and temperature differed.

Reading the Evidence

Job · Map · Evidence · Limit · Answer

明确题目问什么；看该段在全文中的作用；找决定性的英文证据；检查范围、程度与时间；最后比较答案。

1. What was observed, and what was inferred?

Focus: observation and explanation. Method: separate the record from its possible cause. Action: underline one observation in paragraph 2 and box Maya’s proposed explanation.

2. Why does the writer include the partner’s comment?

Focus: paragraph function. Method: name the change in reasoning. Action: connect the two changing conditions to the limit on Maya’s conclusion; compare the options below.

Worked example

The main function of the second paragraph is to

  • (A) describe how a new instrument was invented
  • (B) show why an observation did not establish its proposed cause
  • (C) prove that temperature caused the green specks to increase
  • (D) explain why the first drawing was inaccurate
  • (E) report the successful completion of a repeated experiment

答案 B。Job：paragraph_function。Map：P1观察，P2检查解释，P3改进计划。Evidence：P2两项条件改变；P3 had not yet。Limit：观察和计划都不等于已证实因果。Answer：检查全部选项。

  • A：没有发明新仪器。
  • B：前半报告观察，后半指出两个变量同时改变，限制因果解释。
  • C：指出另一个变量并不证明它就是原因。
  • D：原文没有否定 drawing 的准确性。
  • E：重复实验只在后段计划，尚未完成。

最近干扰：C：排除单一归因不等于证实另一原因。
