课堂词汇笔记 · Meaning in context

先看词性、搭配和句子中的任务，再选准确词义。点击词条展开释义与例句。

turbulent (adj.)

moving irregularly or violently; unsettled

科学语境中可指水流、气流紊乱；不能只套用飞机“颠簸”。

The turbulent water mixed the two samples.

classify (v.)

to arrange things in groups according to shared features

按共同特征分类；区别于 magnify（放大）和 preserve（保存）。

The students classified the fragments by shape.

preserve (v.)

to keep something intact or prevent it from being lost

保留、保存原有状态。

The drawing preserved a record of the observation.

alter (v.)

to change something

改变；detect 是察觉已有事物，不是改变它。

A change in temperature could alter the result.

combine / integrate (v.)

combine: bring together; integrate: bring parts into a working whole

integrate 更强调各部分整合成整体。

The report integrated several observations into one explanation.

replicate / replica (v. / n.)

replicate: repeat a procedure or reproduce a result; replica: a copy of an object

replicate the procedure 是重复实验步骤；a replica 是复制品，本身不等于带欺骗目的的赝品。

The team replicated the experiment. The museum displayed a replica of the instrument.

accidental (adj.)

happening by chance or without intention

偶然的、意外的；不等于每次都能重复得到的。

The researchers tested whether the difference was accidental.

contradictory / consistent (adj.)

contradictory: incompatible or opposed; consistent: agreeing or showing a stable pattern

contradictory 相互矛盾；consistent 一致、保持同样模式。

The two accounts were contradictory, but repeated measurements showed a consistent pattern.

definitive / tentative (adj.)

definitive: conclusive; tentative: provisional and open to revision

definitive 已足以下定论；tentative 仍是暂定的。

Without a controlled comparison, the explanation remained tentative.

ensure / infer (v.)

ensure: make certain; infer: reach a conclusion from evidence

ensure 是确保；infer 是依据证据推断。观察到差异不能直接确保因果。

Equal-sized drops ensure a fairer count; they do not let us infer the cause with certainty.

长句笔记 · Find the core, then restore the logic

先找主语和谓语，再确认宾语；把修饰暂放旁边后，要加回原因、条件和时间关系。

Because the jars had received different amounts of light and had been kept at different temperatures, the comparison could not separate the effects of the two conditions.

Subject: the comparison

Verb: could not separate

Object: the effects （核心名词是 effects；of the two conditions 说明是哪两项条件的影响。）

Because clause: 光照量和温度都不同，说明为什么无法分离两种影响。

Meaning: The comparison could not isolate the effect of light because temperature also differed.

had received / had been kept: had + past participle 表示过去某一时点之前的情况；had been kept 同时是被动，表示瓶子被放在不同温度下。

Reading habit: 暂时划分主干与修饰，是为了看清关系；最终理解仍要保留 because、not yet、would 等词传递的信息。

The Second Sample

At a school science club, Maya collected a 1[ theory / specimen / procedure ] → specimen of pond water in a 2[ transparent / turbulent / reluctant ] → transparent jar. The water looked almost clear, but a microscope could 3[ classify / preserve / magnify ] → magnify details that her eyes alone could not 4[ produce / detect / alter ] → detect. Before offering an explanation, she drew what she saw and recorded where the sample had been collected. The drawing preserved an observation; it did not yet explain why the specks were there.

Maya then divided the water between two matching jars. One stood beside a bright window, while the other was kept on a cooler shelf. After several days, the equal-sized drops from the window jar contained more green specks than those from the shelf jar. Maya wanted to 6[ deny / ensure / infer ] → infer that stronger light had caused the difference. Her partner pointed out that temperature was also a 7[ conclusion / variable / instrument ] → variable. Because the jars had received different amounts of light and had been kept at different temperatures, the comparison could not separate the effects of the two conditions. An accurate count could still be followed by an uncertain explanation.

The group planned another comparison. Water from one well-mixed sample would be divided among several matching jars. The jars that received less light would be kept at the same temperature as the others. Equal-sized drops would be examined at the same time each day. The group would also 8[ replicate / abandon / simplify ] → replicate the procedure with a fresh sample to see whether the pattern was 9[ accidental / contradictory / consistent ] → consistent. These steps had not yet been carried out; therefore, Maya described her explanation as 10[ definitive / irrelevant / tentative ] → tentative. She could report the difference she had observed, but she could not yet name its cause with confidence. Better instruments could improve her observations; a better comparison was still needed to test her explanation.
