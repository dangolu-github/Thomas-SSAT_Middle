先分清范围：Minority Literature ≠ Immigrant Literature

课堂中提到 Asian Americans、African Americans、Latino Americans 和 Native Americans，是为了理解北美文学中的少数群体与身份议题。这个范围比“移民文学”更广。

关键区别：移民文学通常围绕跨地区或跨国家迁移及其后续经验展开；少数群体文学还可能关注族群、历史、权力、文化记忆与代表性。Native American literature 不是移民文学；African American literature 也不能自动归入移民文学。判断时要看作品本身写的是什么经历。

常见主题：从离开到归属

不要只背抽象主题词。阅读时把主题放进“处境—压力—回应—结果”的发展链。

段落地图：Event → Emotion → Action → Theme

Roots and displacementMina 的家庭迁到 Harbor City。机会与失落同时存在；蓝色布、亲属来信和 diaspora 把她与 Aru 连接起来。
Quiet alienation同学没有公开敌意，但说话快、默认共同知识、很少询问 Aru。Mina 因此担心自己的经历被当成不重要的边缘内容。
Turning point: shared activity“Routes to Our Room” 让每个人把一个物件、短语或记忆放进共同地图。私人历史开始进入公共空间。
Participation without erasureMina 逐渐适应并通过贡献融入班级；她没有被要求抹去 Aru，而是把原有身份转化为对他人有用的分享。
Belonging through respect班级的凝聚力来自认真对待不同历史，而不是让每个人拥有同一种历史。文章由个人变化走向群体主题。

长句拆解：先找主干，再看补充关系

Mina worried that speaking about her past would invite others to marginalize her, treating her experience as peripheral to the class.

Main spineMina worried 是句子主干。后面的 `that` 从句说明她担心的内容。
Gerund phrasespeaking about her past 在 `that` 从句中作主语：说起自己的过去这件事。
Verb patterninvite + others + to marginalize 是“动词 + 宾语 + to-infinitive”。这里的 `to marginalize` 补全 `invite`，不是 `in order to`。
-ing phrasetreating her experience as peripheral 说明边缘化的方式或结果；逻辑执行者是 `others`。被动对照：`Her experience was treated as peripheral.`

主题证据 / 关键变化句法与词义细节转折活动

When Mina's family arrived in Harbor City by steamship in 1912, adults described the change as an opportunity. Mina understood that description, yet she also experienced a sense of : the move from the island of Aru had separated her from familiar routines and from relatives who had remained behind. Her grandmother's blue cloth, embroidered with waves, represented the family's , while letters from cousins across several countries reminded Mina that she belonged to a wider .

At the crowded schoolhouse, the problem was not open hostility. It was quieter. Classmates spoke rapidly, assumed shared knowledge of local customs, and rarely asked about Aru. This produced , a feeling of distance even while surrounded by people. Mina worried that speaking about her past would invite others to her, treating her experience as peripheral to the class.

Mr. Chen then introduced “Routes to Our Room.” Each student placed a thread on a wall map and attached one object, phrase, or memory. Mina added the cloth. A classmate displayed a train ticket; another pinned up a recipe written in two languages. The collection turned private histories into a shared record.

As Mina learned the classroom's routines, she began to . The project also helped her , because she was contributing to the group rather than merely observing it. Mr. Chen explained that to can imply becoming so similar to the surrounding culture that visible differences fade. The class did not ask Mina to erase Aru. Instead, it gave her a reason to part of that identity and make it useful to others.

By Friday, the display had made the class more . Mina's strongest new was not based on everyone sharing one history; it came from taking one another's histories seriously. The map suggested that a community can absorb change without demanding sameness.

SSAT Reading：五步锁定证据

题目会换角度，但判断流程不变。先做证据工作，再比较五个选项。

JOB题目要你找 main idea、detail、inference、purpose、tone，还是 word in context？
SCOPE证据范围是一句话、一个段落，还是全文？
EVIDENCE圈出能直接支持判断的词、动作或转折。
